Field Briefing

Study your tools and understand what you're up against. Every BMP tower and every pollutant is real engineering and real science used in Georgia today.

Your mission: Protect the creek at the bottom of the slope. Runoff flows down the path — place BMP towers to intercept it. You start with only $100 — just enough for 2 silt fences. You earn money by intercepting particles during waves. Harder pollutants pay more. Use your earnings to unlock and place more powerful towers. Build smart and build early.
